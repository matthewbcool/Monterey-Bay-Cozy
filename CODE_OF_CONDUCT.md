# Community conduct

This project follows Monterey Bay Tech's people-first, inclusive spirit. People of every experience level are welcome, including those contributing local knowledge, ideas, research or feedback rather than code.

Be respectful and patient. Offer specific, constructive feedback, credit contributions and sources, and help newcomers learn. Respect privacy and ask before sharing someone else's personal information, photographs or messages. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberately disruptive behavior are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to keep the space supportive. For a conduct concern, contact the project maintainer privately through the Monterey Bay Tech Discord server; do not post private reports or personal details in public issues. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and communicate practical next steps to the people involved.

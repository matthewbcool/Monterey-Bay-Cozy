# Lovers Point recreation area

The October 25 pass uses the six user-supplied street-view screenshots to improve the pool, sand volleyball court, cypress apron and adjacent Coastal Trail. Images 3 and 4 repeat the same view. They are visual references, not survey data or runtime textures. The October 26 follow-up uses `active-park-with-loads.jpg` and `looking-west.jpg` supplied by the user to refine the café, beach masonry and pier details.

Open **Testing Gym → Menu → Pool & volleyball court**. Approach the gate and press **E**, gamepad **X**, or tap the interaction prompt. Open it, walk onto the deck, and close it from either side once clear of the swing. The basin stays display-only; ocean swimming retains its existing controls. Gate state lasts for this loaded Gym and is not saved to an avatar or shared over a network. Map Study displays the same scenery with its gate closed.

## October 26 café and beach details

The Grill retains OSM footprint `way/224525166`, with an authored 2.45 m wall height and 0.35 m blue-green roof rise replacing the generic 3.5 m building mass. Cream walls, recessed serving windows, a warm counter and burgundy roof sign follow the first photo. Sign lettering is batched geometry. Four 2.5 m diameter red/pink parasols, 1.06 m tables and eight chairs occupy the nearby paved apron, clear of mapped pedestrian centerlines. Furniture has low solid collision proxies; it is decorative rather than an interaction or café simulation.

The existing **8.25 m authored recreation terrace** extends beneath the café and seating. This corrects the coarse DEM's uphill mound through the kiosk while preserving the mapped footprint and original source JSON. The same graded grid supplies ground rendering, furniture placement and walking. The paved apron is partitioned into the existing ground surface, never layered over it.

Beach wall runs bend more gradually and share sloping cap heights instead of individual flat-topped boxes. Seeded granite color fields remain inexpensive geometry. Slender oriented collision strips follow the wall and leave mapped stairs clear. The pier gains 1 m dark posts and two horizontal rails following its existing slab heights, with an open terminal for boat access. Its mapped utility hut (`way/931232626`) now has a provisional 2.3 m cream wall and nearly flat 0.12 m roof rise. The full stepped quay, lower beach terraces and their real elevations still need a measured profile; this pass does not claim to reconstruct them exactly.

All components are shared by Gym and Map Study. Closed building shells omit roof-covered caps and buried foundation bottoms; do not reuse that optimization for a building with a visible interior. Wall strips likewise omit buried bottoms and adjoining end faces. These savings pay for the added details within the existing park ceiling. ## October 26 height and layout correction

The user's `img_0386.jpg` and `Lovers-Point-Volleyball.jpg`, checked against PDF pages **33**, **44** and **53** of the [City of Pacific Grove facilities assessment](https://files.cityofpacificgrove.org/Document_Center/Departments/Public%20Works/Buildings%20%26%20Grounds/Lovers%20Point%20Park.pdf?t=202510301223500), show the inland retaining wall, the lower pool/court promenade and the Beach House's ground-floor entrances. The first October 26 correction raised every side of the facilities to the bike-trail datum; that surrounding bank and its screenshots are superseded.

The mapped footprints remain fixed: **old bathhouse / Beach House → pool → volleyball court → Grill**, progressing south along the waterfront. The change corrects vertical relationships rather than moving the buildings or court. The Grill and café apron remain connected to the lower recreation terrace. The Beach House's visible ground floor uses an **8.31 m** datum, 0.06 m above the terrace, with a continuous **2.5 m** paved approach along the stepped waterfront frontage, while its rear meets the inland hillside; the highest point under its footprint no longer lifts its entire front onto a tall plinth.

The retained **8.25 m** lower terrace is informed by raw DEM samples near the pool and court of approximately **8.61 m** and **8.06 m**. Raw DEM values at the modeled bike-trail endpoints are approximately **12.34 m** and **11.44 m**, supporting retention of its level **11.5 m** study datum. The intermediate **9.85 m** court-facing apron and **1.6 m** retaining rise are inferred from photographs, not surveyed levels. The terrain transitions inland toward the bike trail and descends seaward without the former raised rim.

The beach stair beside the court, OSM **`way/633871934`**, is widened from **2.1 m to a provisional 4 m** along its existing mapped alignment. Its treads, landing, rails and walking clearance follow that wider footprint. The corrected grade seats the stair at approximately **8.29 m** at the terrace and **3.94 m** at the beach, with less than 0.12 m landing clearance across the width. These are authored geometry measurements, not surveyed elevations. This is separate from the distant east-beach access, `way/633871947`; the court reference does not establish a change to that staircase. Exact tread counts, widths, elevations and building datums remain authored approximations pending a measured section.

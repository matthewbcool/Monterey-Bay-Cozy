# Gray whales in the Testing Gym

The Gym has a **one-in-five chance per scene load** of staging a gray-whale gathering well offshore of Lovers Point: a 12.5 m mother, her 5.7 m calf, and another 11.8 m adult. **Menu → Whale lookout** frames the offshore water and reports when no whales are visible. A successful sighting stays in place for that visit; the pod never blinks in or out during play. When present, the whales travel as a compact group, leave a subtle shared-batch wake, exhale visible spouts, sometimes lift their flukes before a brief dive, and rarely breach with a low-poly splash. They are not added to the Tank Demo; the reusable [gray-whale factory](../src/models/gray-whale/) remains the only model source.

This is an **authored scene**, not a live sighting or a prediction of when whales will visit. [NOAA Fisheries](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/gray-whale) describes gray whales as often traveling alone or in small unstable groups and records mothers with single calves on their northbound coastal migration. The [Monterey Bay Aquarium](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/gray-whale) reports that mother-calf pairs are commonly seen in Monterey Bay in spring and gives a 5–6 m calf size by the time they pass through. [National Park Service whale-watching guidance](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/learn/nature/whale-watching.htm) describes tail flukes rising before a whale dives and breaches as occasional. The chosen animals, loop, breathing periods and event timing are game-art decisions. The Gym's weather clock does not alter their presence.

`src/viewer/testingGym/whalePod.ts` defines the 20% sighting gate and, when successful, owns the three model instances, surface effects, deterministic clock, visibility radius and teardown. The roll happens once when the Gym is created. On the other 80% of loads, no whale meshes, effects or LOD buffers are created. `createTestingGym` accepts a supplied roll so a future shared-world authority can give all clients the same result; the current Gym uses a local roll. Their metre-scale roots and existing capsule physics descriptors remain unscaled; the animation only places path-owned rigid roots and advances the factory's vertical-fluke skeleton. If a future physics owner takes one root, the pod stops repositioning it. The calf follows beside and slightly behind the mother, and the other adult travels about 13–15 m from her. The route remains more than 100 m from the mapped coast at tested positions. The adults now breach roughly every 220–260 seconds on offset schedules, instead of roughly every 86–94 seconds. Mother and calf sound nearly together; the other adult has its own dive cycle. Each nine-second tail-first dive is a shallow visual gesture because the Gym seabed cannot represent a real gray whale's deeper, longer dives. No whale collision or network simulation is claimed. Reduced-motion mode freezes travel and surface events; the viewer's shared LOD controller independently selects Near/Mid/Far on both graphics backends.

An absent pod costs **zero triangles and zero draws**. A visible resting pod costs **4,566 / 2,266 / 912 triangles** at Near/Mid/Far in **seven draws**: six whale model draws and one three-instance wake draw. Spray and splash geometry only renders during events, under a **5,200-triangle / 13-draw** pod ceiling. When present, the three LOD families plus inactive effect geometry occupy **355,244 typed-array bytes**; this is not measured GPU memory. A 290 m plan-view interest radius hides the pod when the player is far away, while its world clock keeps advancing independently of visibility. These counts exclude the park, water, atmosphere, other coastal life and renderer overhead.

`tests/gym-whale-pod.test.ts` checks sighting odds, absent costs, scale, offshore clearance, group spacing, tail exposure, rare breaches, budgets, reduced motion, interest management and LOD. `tests/gym-whale-pod-browser.mjs` checks both sighting outcomes in the actual Gym, both rendering backends, all detail levels, the Whale lookout, surface effects and browser errors. The development preview accepts `?tab=gym&whales=show` or `?tab=gym&whales=hide` to inspect either outcome without rerolling; those overrides are disabled in production. Visual evidence and costs (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot) are local browser captures, not a physical-phone performance measurement.

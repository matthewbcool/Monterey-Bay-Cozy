# Caledonia Park and neighborhood

Caledonia is a shared procedural district in Map Study and the Testing Gym. Use **Testing Gym → Menu → Destination → Caledonia Park**, or open `?tab=gym&destination=caledonia-park`. The destination name persists while walking, **Back to start** returns to its park gate, and its overview frames the whole park and nearby community center. The separate **Meals on Wheels center** shortcut reaches the mapped forecourt. **Map Study → Caledonia Park · top view** shows the same geometry. North-up views include projected place/street labels. Normal walking, running and Otter Man flight share ground and collision data.

## Geography and references

- The [retained OSM request](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-121.9223,36.6202,-121.9182,36.6246) was downloaded October 25, 2026. © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. The derived [cache](../src/data/caledonia-park.json) contains 347 clipped route segments and 220 clipped building parts.
- Caledonia Park is OSM `way/871779748`; Tommy Stillwell Court is `way/435247876`; playgrounds are `way/633507463` and `way/633507464`. Their boundaries and orientation come from those records, not tracing the perspective photo.
- Streets include Caledonia, Central, Pacific, Union, 19th and 20th, with the Jewell connection at the eastern edge. OSM calls the curved road Caledonia Street; the city's park description and the supplied aerial use Caledonia Avenue. The plan labels use Avenue.
- [City recreation catalog](https://secure.rec1.com/CA/pacific-grove-ca/catalog/index?filter=dGFiJTVCNzkwNiU1RD0xJnNlYXJjaD0mcmVudGFsJTVCZnJvbSU1RD0mcmVudGFsJTVCdG8lNUQ9) and the [historical city park inventory](https://files.cityofpacificgrove.org/Document_Center/Departments/Public%20Works/Buildings%20%26%20Grounds/Parks%20Maintenance/Parks%20Inventory.pdf) support the park's location between Central and Jewell and its basketball, lawn and playground uses. Inventory counts are not a current survey.
- The [city park page](https://www.cityofpacificgrove.gov/our_city/departments/recreation/parks_directory/caledonia_park.php), inspected October 31, 2026, lists 1.69 acres, two age-group play areas, open play, basketball/baseball, picnic tables and restrooms at 161 Caledonia Avenue. Its five small gallery photos guide playground forms and colors. Their URL timestamps are from 2021; capture dates and current equipment condition are unverified. Photo-by-photo observations (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot).
- The user's October 25 aerial and street-level court images inform the two rounded white backboards, curved gray supports, orange rims, court details, benches and broadleaf planting. Photos remain external references, not runtime textures.
- A retained 64 × 72 float raster from [USGS 3DEP](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer) supplies elevations. Its source spacing is approximately 5.72 m east/west and 7.12 m north/south. Resolution does not establish survey accuracy. The roughly 13 m fringe at the northern display crop clamps to the first DEM sample row.

The original district crop is **285 × 360 m**, local X **−430…−145**, Z **90…450**, using the existing origin **−121.91685, 36.6256**, +X east and +Z south. The mapped court footprint is approximately **30.75 × 17.43 m**. These are projected outline dimensions, not certified playing-line dimensions. The cached park polygon measures approximately **5,955.9 m² / 1.472 acres**, about **13% smaller** than the city’s listed **1.69 acres**. The reason for this boundary/area discrepancy is unverified; do not rescale the mapped court, paths or buildings to force the figures to agree. A newer park/parcel plan is needed to resolve it. A separate **95 × 75 m** annex at X **−240…−145**, Z **15…90** covers the real Meals on Wheels site. Its 49 clipped route segments and 24 × 21 USGS raster are retained in [caledonia-meals-site.json](../src/data/caledonia-meals-site.json). The annex uses NAVD88 heights, joins the old terrain at the east/south edges and preserves the unmodeled northwest notch. The finer source spacing does not imply surveyed construction accuracy.

## Construction and gameplay

[Factory](../src/viewer/mapStudy/caledonia.ts): `createCaledoniaArea(origin?)`. Public geometry uses metres and the root stays scale one. Map Study's latitude adjustment is baked into vertices. Returned walking and flight descriptors use the canonical Gym coordinate frame, matching the existing waterfront convention.

The source DEM is retained unchanged. A derived 7.5 m mesh grades the court apron to approximately **22.52 m**, blends back into the surrounding DEM, and meets the detailed eastern terrain. Every rendered ground fragment and the controller use the same triangular height sampler. Court, road, sidewalk, lawn and playground finishes partition one surface. Thin painted court markings are deliberately separated above it. Building tops have one roof surface.

The court has two hoops with nominal 3.05 m rims and nine nearby benches. The fence follows the full **455 m mapped park perimeter**, with **five 2.4 m open pedestrian passages** at cached footway contacts. The court is open inside this enclosure. The historical city inventory confirms surrounding fencing and five pedestrian/two utility gates; utility gate locations are not surveyed and are not invented. Fence height **1.8 m**, opening widths and sparse wire spacing are authored estimates, informed by actual park photographs.

The two mapped woodchip beds now contain city-photo-informed blue-post climbing decks, red/yellow rails and panels, a disc climber, red-roofed tan playhouse, blue slides, low twin slide and spring rider. Weathered timber edges follow the ground and leave two open entry gaps (one at the cached footway contact, one inferred); shared edging is emitted once. Equipment assignment within the beds and all dimensions remain provisional. Two picnic tables have provisional lawn positions.

Thirteen seed-251025 shade trees retain their placement with lower-triangle crowns to fund the equipment detail inside the existing district budget. Paths added along the lawn are photo-informed approximations. The court-to-park connector ends at the mapped western walkway instead of cutting through the woodchip bed beneath the slides. Street widths, sidewalks, playing lines, fence dimensions, entrances, furniture locations, playground equipment, grading and building heights/roof forms are authored estimates. Untagged building heights default to 5.5 m, except the mapped restroom (`way/633507462`), which uses a provisional single-storey 3.1 m mass. Crop-edge building footprints can be truncated; buildings are exterior massing with no interiors.

The [Meals on Wheels factory](../src/viewer/mapStudy/caledoniaMeals.ts) preserves OSM `way/559348745`, the exact 29-point outline at **700 Jewell Avenue**. Its [official exterior photograph](https://mowcommunitycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PXL_20221202_144239534edit.webp) informs the sage siding, four-post canopy, raised gable, rectangular tower, clerestory glazing, six-pane window and red rails. The southeastern entrance is a footprint/photo interpretation supported by the mapped front footway. Hidden elevations, height, roof pitch and ramp layout remain estimates; this is an exterior study. Inspected photographs, primary source files and uncertainty (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot).

Ground, furniture and building collision are independent of render LOD. Actual fence spans block walking; entrances stay clear for larger avatars. The Gym's walking outline expands only across the new district, and the suit classifies it as dry land. The coarse bay terrain is cut out under the district in both viewers. There is no extra ocean surface, ball simulation, basketball scoring or working playground interaction. Equipment footprints and flight bounds are derived from the source geometry before batching. No accessibility certification is implied by the modeled entry gaps. The baseball backstop, swings, exact picnic layout and restroom facade remain unverified/unmodeled details; they are not silently treated as completed assets.

## Rendering budget and costs

| Detail | Triangles | Asset draws before culling |

| Near | 44,497 | 8 |

The district ceiling is **45,000 triangles / 8 draws / zero image textures**. Four district batches, one annex batch, two fence batches and one building batch use the shared toon materials and LOD controller. The fence has authored **3,582 / 1,914 / 870** triangle variants, dropping wire detail before posts and rails. Meals on Wheels uses **549 / 231 / 165** triangles in one draw; close siding and window divisions drop with distance. Authored LOD families stay outside generic static batching, with projection baked into every variant. Terrain/road batches retain the tight 2 cm simplification tolerance, so most remaining geometry is stable ground and mapped massing. Current geometry storage is recorded in the October 31 review (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot); the October 26 buffer measurements remain in that earlier review as historical evidence. These are buffer counts, not measured GPU-memory allocation. Scene lighting, shadows, other districts and the avatar add separate costs. No per-frame geometry or facility animation is needed; plan labels use DOM text.

The original derived JSON is **127,155 bytes**, **35,475 bytes gzip** (34.64 KiB); the annex adds **17,866 bytes**, **4,267 bytes gzip**. This is the geographic data component, not the entire client download. Procedural code, application code, Three.js and other enabled scenes are additional. Raw OSM and TIFF evidence stays in the repository and is not imported by the browser.

## Maintenance and verification

`python3 scripts/extract-caledonia.py` regenerates the derived cache offline from retained XML/TIFF and the waterfront's building ownership list. `python3 scripts/extract-caledonia-meals.py` regenerates the separate annex from its retained primary sources (use `--output /tmp/caledonia-meals-site.json` to compare without overwriting). Both require Pillow. Public provider requests are authoring-time only. Source evidence and verification (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot) distinguishes the source data from construction choices.

- `tests/caledonia.test.ts`: source IDs/streets, projected dimensions, metre scale, all five gate passages, whole-perimeter collision, one terrain surface, shared floor sampling, dry land and LOD budgets.
- `tests/caledonia-playgrounds.test.ts`: equipment vertices inside mapped beds, grounded posts, upward-facing slide floors/roof pitches, independent collisions, open entries and a bounded equipment triangle count.
- `tests/caledonia-meals.test.ts`: exact building source/placement, actual render LOD ground, dense seam samples, shared toon/one draw, disposal, map reprojection, immutable collisions and dry/walk/flight ownership.
- `tests/caledonia-browser.mjs`: direct destination links, persistent title, destination reset/overview/resize, actual keyboard passage through a park gate, Meals forecourt arrival, WebGPU and WebGL 2, Near/Mid/Far court, building and playground captures, shared Map Study and a 390 px touch/reduced-motion layout.

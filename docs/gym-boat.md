# Testing Gym rowboat

The Testing Gym now places the existing procedural Seafoam rowboat alongside the Timber dock. The hull remains the library's provisional **3.80 m × 1.45 m** design, at its original metre scale. The dock deck is **3.00 m × 1.20 m**. Both are artistic assets, not surveyed boats or structures at Lovers Point. The dock, boarding marker, and mooring are authored placements on the mapped east shoreline; the water display level is an approximate **Y = 1 m NAVD88** reference, not a tide prediction.

Walk to the dock and press **E** or tap **Board the rowboat**. While seated, **W/S** or the up/down touch buttons row forward and back; **A/D** or the left/right buttons steer. Hold **Shift** or toggle **Row faster** for a faster forward stroke. Return close to the mooring and press **E** or tap **Dock & step ashore**. **Back to start** recalls the boat and returns the character to the park entrance from anywhere. Loading another character or leaving the gym also brings the character safely ashore.

`src/viewer/testingGym/boatController.ts` owns the kinematic travel root. The `createRowboat()` factory stays independent of camera, input, and renderer code. Its visual child samples the same wave function as the water shader for small bob and tilt; the avatar's seated root follows the boat's metre-scale bench. Collision checks sample the hull against the mapped shoreline and the playable water bounds before each motion step, so rowing backward stops short of land. The ocean shader draws a subtle trailing wake from the boat’s position, heading and speed; reverse travel reverses it and stopping removes it. This is an analytic visual effect with no persistent trail history or physical force. The same controller now also drives the gym's dive boat with its own hull, berth and speeds; the two boats avoid each other's moving hulls (see [dive site](gym-dive-site.md)). The current controller has no oar strokes, currents, rigid-body dynamics, docking alignment, passengers, or multiplayer synchronization. See the ocean look review (historical authoring evidence omitted from this public snapshot). The boat uses the existing batched asset geometry and materials; this feature adds no new image textures.

`tests/gym-boat.test.ts` checks mooring, land access, motion, steering, collision, reset, and scale. `tests/gym-boat-browser.mjs` checks boarding, rowing, disembarking, keyboard and touch controls, and native WebGPU/WebGL 2 rendering. Browser touch checks emulate a 390 px phone in Chrome on a Mac; physical mobile devices have not been checked.
